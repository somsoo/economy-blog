<h2>The Risks We Need to Talk About</h2>
<p>Now, I’m not here to sell you a fairytale. Every single investment comes with risk, and anyone telling you otherwise is trying to sell you a course. I&#8217;ve made my fair share of mistakes, and I want you to avoid them.</p>
<p>When it comes to <strong>Real World Assets RWA crypto</strong>, you aren&#8217;t just dealing with smart contract risk (though bugs in the code are always a real threat). You are also dealing with legal and regulatory risks. If a token represents a physical warehouse in Ohio, what happens if the local legal system disputes the ownership rights of the digital token holder?</p>
<p>Furthermore, counterparty risk is massive here. You rely on trusted custodians to hold the physical assets safely. If the company managing the gold or the real estate goes belly-up or turns out to be fraudulent, your on-chain token might just be a digital receipt for a headache. Always look for projects that undergo rigorous, transparent third-party audits and have bulletproof legal frameworks.</p>

<h2>What is Real World Asset (RWA) Tokenization and How Does It Work?</h2>
<p>Let’s break it down without the boring Wall Street jargon. Simply put, Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization takes tangible, high-value assets and slices them into fractional, digital tokens on-chain. Think of it like dividing a massive, expensive gourmet pizza into thousands of accessible slices.</p>
<p>Instead of needing millions of dollars to acquire a commercial office building in Manhattan, an investor can purchase a fractionalized piece on-chain for a fraction of the cost. Major institutional heavyweights like BlackRock are diving headfirst into this space, proving that Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization is the future of global finance, making transactions faster, cheaper, and infinitely more transparent.</p>
